About Mr. Arun Kanagasabapathy

Mr. Arun Kanagasabapathy is a highly accomplished Civil Engineer and Real Estate Valuer with a career spanning more than 25 years. Representing a standard of professional rigor, his practice integrates core civil engineering diagnostics with high-tier financial-legal valuations.

He is a Registered Valuer under the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) for wealth tax evaluation and assets coming under the scrutiny of the Black Money Act. Additionally, he holds certifications under the Companies Act 2013, enabling corporate asset valuation.

An active academic and educator in the field, Mr. Arun has co-authored 9 authoritative books on practical valuation and has published 10 technical papers in the prestigious Indian Valuer journal. His expertise is widely sought after in national forums, having attended over 70 professional conferences in India and key international conventions, including the Landmark Valuation Summit in Bangkok.
